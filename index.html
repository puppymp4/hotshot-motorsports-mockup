What We Do

Six lanes. One shop.
Stock to stage in one stop.

Most shops do one thing well. We do six, under one roof, with the same crew. Your truck does not need a tour of the Central Valley. It needs a parking spot at 3770 N Marty.

01

Lift Kits & Suspension

Zero to 13 inches. Anylevel, Cognito, McGaughys, FTS, Full Throttle, Stryker. From a clean leveling kit to a 12-inch show stance, we spec it, install it, dial in the geometry, and align it.

02

Wheels & Tires

Up to 30x16 forged. Hostile, JTX, KG1, VT, KMC, Specialty Forged, American Force. Mounted, balanced, and torqued in-house. Fury, Toyo, Venom rubber up to 42 inches.

03

Custom Lighting

Headlight retrofits, switchback DRLs, color-deleted ambers, underbody RGB, 30-piece rock light kits, switchback door handles. We build the harnesses. Nothing is plug-and-pray.

04

Wraps & Color Match

Full-body satin, gloss, and chrome wraps. OEM-precise color match on bumpers, fenders, and faceplates. Smoothed plastics. Painted bedsides. Mirrors that disappear.

05

Audio & Interior

Custom door panels, fiberglass sub enclosures, dual-12 stealth boxes, 1500-piece starlight headliners, Roadwire interiors. Sound that punches without rattling the dash.

06

Full SEMA Builds

Concept to keys. We have shipped F450 Limiteds, Denali Ultimates, and Anylevel show trucks straight from the dealer to the floor at SEMA. If you can dream it, we have probably already built one harder.

How It Works

From DM to driveway.
No mystery. No middleman.

Most shops vanish after the deposit. We send updates. We answer the phone. You will know exactly where your truck is from drop-off to delivery.

01

Talk To Us

Call, DM, or walk in. Tell us the truck and the dream. We listen first, then we tell you what is realistic, what will turn out best, and what to skip.

02

Spec The Build

We write up the parts list and the timeline. Honest pricing. No hidden line items. You sign off before a wrench moves.

03

The Shop Takes Over

You get progress photos as we go. Suspension, wheels, paint, lighting, audio, the chrome. Every step done in-house, by our crew, on your truck.

04

You Drive Out Different

Final reveal. Walkaround. Care instructions. Then you take the keys back and watch the parking lot go quiet when you pull up.
